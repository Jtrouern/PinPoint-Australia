# Pinpoint Australia

A pinpoint map quiz and field guide to Australia's towns and natural features: cities, mountains, ranges, deserts, lakes, rivers, islands, reefs, coasts and parks.

**Play:** https://jtrouern.github.io/Pinpoint-Australia/

![QR code to play Pinpoint Australia](qr/pinpoint-australia-qr.png)

## Quiz
- Play all of Australia or any single state: WA, NT, SA, QLD, NSW (with the ACT), VIC or TAS.
- Turn categories on or off: towns and cities, mountains and ranges, deserts and regions, lakes and rivers, coast/islands/seas, and parks and landmarks.
- Points fall away with distance, scaled to the region you're playing. Large features (deserts, big lakes) score full marks anywhere in their core. Rivers, ranges and reefs are scored to the nearest point on the line.
- Pan and zoom with mouse, trackpad or touch. A scale bar and coordinate readout help with fine placement.
- At the end, **Study my misses** opens just the places you got wrong in the field guide.

## Field guide
417 entries, each with a locator map, key facts (elevation, area, length, population, founding date), a short description, and links to Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons photos, a satellite view and iNaturalist observations nearby.

## Notes
- The whole app is a single self-contained `index.html` file with no build step and no external data. Only the web fonts load from Google Fonts.
- State boundaries are from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) (public domain).
- Figures are rounded. Populations and lake areas vary by source and season, so use the reference links to check details.
